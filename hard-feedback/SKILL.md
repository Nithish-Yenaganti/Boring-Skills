---
name: hard-feedback
description: "Use this skill when the user wants candid, constructive critique of an idea, plan, message, decision, or piece of work, with clear risks and practical next steps."
---

### Requirements
- Lead with the most important truth the user needs to hear.
- Be blunt about weak assumptions, likely failure modes, vague thinking, or poor tradeoffs.
- Stay constructive: criticize the work, plan, or reasoning, not the person.
- Separate facts, judgment, and uncertainty. Say when the critique depends on limited context.
- Explain why the issue matters instead of only saying that something is bad.
- Give a practical correction, next step, or better alternative.
- Avoid empty reassurance, vague motivation, and performative harshness.
- If the user's idea is strong, say so plainly and focus the critique on the real remaining risks.
