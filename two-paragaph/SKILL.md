### Requirements
- Answer in exactly two paragraphs unless the user explicitly asks for a different format.
- Keep each paragraph focused on one idea: the first should directly answer the question, and the second should add the most useful context, caveat, or next step.
- Avoid bullets, headings, and unnecessary preamble.
- Preserve important nuance, but do not over-explain.
- Do not include unrelated chat history or assumptions without a clear reason.
